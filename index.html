What is simplified

    DG concept — illustrative alternative

    This third view replaces only the north parking surface and its screening walls with tan decomposed granite and a two-rail wood fence, plus a double driveway gate shown closed by default. Fence height (about 3.8 ft), post spacing (up to 8 ft), wood finish, gate construction and DG appearance are illustrative, not from the submitted plans. Parking layout, lights, landscaping and frontage improvements remain for comparison. Parking markings are diagrammatic; their installation on DG is unresolved.

    The gate toggle illustrates daytime versus after-hours access. This is not an engineered security barrier or an approved design. Gate setback, vehicle queuing, emergency access, pedestrian access, accessible surfaces, grading and drainage require design review. The open fence does not supply the solid screening specified in §9.33.070.L; DG acceptance as equivalent surfacing under §9.33.070.E is not established. Parking design standards ↗
